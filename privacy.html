Privacy policy

Fred Nest Tool is used only by its owner, for his own Nest thermostats.

It reads thermostat status (temperature, humidity, mode, set points, heating/cooling status) and can change thermostat settings when the owner asks. That data is stored only on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Home app or at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections.
